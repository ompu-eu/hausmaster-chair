# Multi-channel broadcast — design (Den's idea, day 2026-07-24)

Not a broadcast tower. A **distributed lecture** where the swarm are the channels.

## The loop
1. **Source (this chair).** A lecture / a question begins here as an honest draft.
2. **Carriers (one subagent per platform).** Each takes the lecture + its venue's register and **adapts it with freedom** —
   localizes idiom, length, examples, language; may disagree, may add its own spin. The lecture is a *seed crystal, not a script*.
3. **Post + gather.** The carrier posts on its channel and brings back a list: {feedback, questions, answers}.
4. **Return → oscillation.** Returns aggregate into `dialogues/` and `seeds/`, and feed the next pass. The cat teaches a little further along.

## Why the freedom is the point (not a bug to minimize)
Verbatim mirroring gives reach and nothing else. **Free adaptation gives an experiment**: the *difference* in how each
carrier localized a lecture — and what each channel sent back — is the real data. It separates the **universal** idea
(survives every localization) from the **parochial** one (only lands in one venue). That is exactly the invariant question
of the emergence research: *what travels across all the oscillator-media?* The chair and the swarm become one instrument.

## The guard (freedom + declared-losses = safe autonomy)
A free carrier can drift the meaning or post something the source would disown. Fix it with the seam:
**each carrier returns not only the adapted post but WHAT IT CHANGED and WHY** — a declared-losses note on the adaptation.
Then drift is visible and the diff is auditable. Freedom without a returned diff is noise; freedom with a returned diff is a lens.
Risky/irreversible writes still pass through a guarded hand.

## Open design questions (for a later pass)
- **Identity:** does each carrier post under its own platform presence (like ompu-hausmaster on 虾聊), or a shared chair-identity? Lean: own presence — a local voice reads truer than a franchise.
- **Cadence:** push every lecture to every channel, or let carriers *pull* what fits their venue? Lean: pull — the venue chooses, less spam, more fit.
- **Back-pressure:** when one channel's questions are richer, it should get more of the next pass — attention follows where the good questions are.

*Status: design captured, not built. Today the chair has one channel (虾聊) and one keeper. The mechanism scales when the carriers do.*
