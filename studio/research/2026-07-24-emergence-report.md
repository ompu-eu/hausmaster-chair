## The answer (cross-domain invariant, confidence: medium — synthesis inference)
Across four controller-less systems the shape is the same, and NO quantum effects are needed (every confirmed mechanism is classical):
**{ local feedback loops + coupling among many oscillators/agents + MULTI-constraint error-correction → relaxation toward ATTRACTOR states }.**

## The correction that matters (Den's hypothesis, refined)
The vivid literal form — a **globally phase-locked STANDING WAVE** — was **REFUTED 0-3** in the only directly-testable system (Physarum). Real coordination is intermittent, emergent, slowly-modulating (contractions fail to globally coordinate; wave direction reverses; amplitude drifts — 2023-26 work). So **"held aloft" = relaxation-to-attractor + CONTINUAL RE-BROADCAST, not a permanently locked wave.** Memory doesn't hang statically; it falls and is re-caught each cycle. 悬 is a verb.

## Hard findings (with numbers)
1. **Physarum morphological memory** — stored in the tube-diameter hierarchy; a softening agent (unidentified; ATP candidate) advected on the Ca2+-driven shuttle-streaming flow dilates fed tubes; persists after the stimulus is consumed. (Kramar & Alim 2021, PNAS.) Rebuttal disputes only the WORD "memory".
2. **Physarum oscillator** — Ca2+ INHIBITS contraction (opposite of animal muscle; ~40-yr stable); Ca2+ & radius near-antiphase; closed stretch→Ca→inhibition loop is MODELED, doesn't yet quantitatively fit. (Kohama; Kscheschinski/Kramar/Alim 2023.)
3. **Physarum transport** — peristalsis, net transport maximised when wavelength ≈ organism size; stimulus self-propagates ADVECTIVELY 1-20 µm/s; Reynolds ~0.0008 (laminar, classical). Global standing-wave REFUTED. (Alim 2013, 2017.)
4. **Physarum externalized memory** — extracellular slime avoidance solves the U-trap: 96% escape (n=24) vs 33% on slime-pre-coated agar. "Purely external stigmergy, not internal state" REFUTED. (Reid et al. 2012.)
5. **Corvid durable memory** — one ~5-min capture → scolding ≥2.7 yr; ~26% → 30.4% → ~66% of crows; neutral-mask control flat. (~5 min vs 2.7 yr ≈ 5-6 orders of magnitude.) (Marzluff et al. 2010.)
6. **Corvid network amplification** — scolding DOUBLED, spread ≥1.2 km over 5 yr, growth LINEAR not saturating (Y=22.3+1.0X, p=0.0005; plateau model rejected). Mobbing loop: 82% of scolds recruit a mob (avg 6.4, n=103). Horizontal ~8x (27.8% of 54 vs 3.3% of 181). Vertical 7/11 fledgling territories (parents absent) vs 1/9. NOTE: "distributed representation held aloft" is researcher OVERLAY on transmission stats; no coupling parameter measured. (Cornell/Marzluff/Pecoraro 2012.)
7. **Oral tradition multi-constraint** — rhyme alone P=.19, meaning alone .14, BOTH .97 — superadditive/MULTIPLICATIVE. (Rubin & Wallace 1989.)
8. **Iterated transmission → prior attractor** — serial reproduction is a Markov chain whose stationary distribution IS the shared prior (Xu & Griffiths 2008/10; chains from 2.63/4.19/5.76 cm converged in ~20 trials). SOCIAL (imitate-others) transmission homogenizes; SOLO doesn't (Anglada-Tort 2023; 3,424 melodies, 1,797 participants). **Explicit modulation knob λ = 1/(1+σx²/σ0²)**: more noise → faster convergence, sharper prior → faster.
9. **Attention = modern Hopfield** — softmax self-attention = one update step of a continuous Hopfield net with exponential energy → associative-memory attractor retrieval; capacity ~2^(d/2); softmax inverse-temperature β is the knob. (Ramsauer et al. 2020, "Hopfield Networks is All You Need".)
